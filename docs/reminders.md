# Reminders

The **Reminders** screen lists what needs attention today. It is found under
**Home** in the menu, and behind the bell at the top of every screen. The
number on the bell is the count of open reminders. Home shows the same in a
short strip when there is something to look at.

Reminders are worked out fresh each time a screen opens. Nothing has to be
ticked off: a reminder goes away by itself once the customer pays or the part
is back in stock.

## Customers who have not paid

A customer is listed when a bill of theirs has been unpaid for 15 days or more.

- The number of days can be changed in **Settings → Reminders**.
- A bill with its own due date (set from the customer's payment terms) is
  listed from the day after that date instead.
- Payments are not tied to single bills, so money received is counted against
  the customer's oldest bills first. The amount shown is only the late part of
  what they owe; the full balance is shown beside it when it is larger.
- An opening balance is counted from the day the customer was added.
- Cash bills and cancelled bills are never counted.

Each customer has three buttons: **Call**, **WhatsApp** (opens a chat with a
polite message filled in; nothing is sent until you press send) and **Receive
payment**.

Only the owner sees this list. Staff see low stock only.

## Low stock

A part is listed when:

- it is at or below its own minimum (set on the part's Items page), or
- it is out of stock and has been stocked or sold before, or
- its stock is below zero.

**Settings → Reminders → Low stock level for all parts** sets one level for
every part that has no minimum of its own. It only applies to parts that have
been stocked or sold before, so parts never kept in the shop do not fill the
list. Leave it at 0 to turn it off. To keep one part out of the list, set that
part's own minimum to 0.

The screen shows the first ten parts; **See all** opens the full Low Stock
report.

## What it does not do

Reminders show inside the app. The app does not send a text message, an email
or a phone notification when it is closed.
